Betty Client

Coming soon

A lightweight launcher and client for Minecraft: Java Edition 1.8.9, built for smooth gameplay on everyday PCs.

Un launcher e client leggero per Minecraft: Java Edition 1.8.9, pensato per giocare fluido anche su PC senza scheda video dedicata. In arrivo.

Official Microsoft sign-in

Players sign in with their own Microsoft account. Betty never sees passwords and does not support offline or unlicensed accounts.

Official game files

Minecraft is downloaded only from Mojang's servers and every file is verified. Betty only adds its own client-side features.

Your data stays on your PC

Login tokens are stored encrypted on the user's computer (Windows DPAPI). No ads, no tracking.

Betty Client is an independent project. Not an official Minecraft product. Not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
