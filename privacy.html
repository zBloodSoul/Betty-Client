Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Betty Client ("Betty") is a desktop launcher and client for Minecraft: Java Edition 1.8.9. This policy explains what data Betty handles and where it goes. In short: Betty has no servers, collects nothing, and keeps your sign-in on your own PC.

1. Microsoft account sign-in

When you sign in, you enter your credentials on Microsoft's official sign-in page. Betty never sees or stores your password. After sign-in, Betty receives tokens from Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services, which are used only to verify that you own Minecraft and to launch the game.

2. Data stored on your computer

  • Your sign-in refresh token, encrypted with Windows DPAPI so that only your Windows user can read it. Signing out deletes it.
  • Your Minecraft username, UUID and skin, used to show your profile in the launcher.
  • Launcher and client settings, HUD layouts, profiles and cosmetics you choose.
  • Log files of the launcher and the game, kept locally to help you troubleshoot.

None of this data leaves your computer, except when you choose to share it (for example by sending a log file when asking for help).

3. Services Betty connects to

  • Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services, to sign you in and check game ownership.
  • Mojang's download servers, to download and verify the game, its libraries and assets.
  • Adoptium (Eclipse Foundation), to download the Java runtime required by the game, if it is not already installed.

These services process requests under their own privacy policies. Betty does not send them any data beyond what is needed for these requests.

4. What Betty does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No accounts on our side and no Betty servers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

5. Deleting your data

Sign out in the launcher to delete your sign-in token. Uninstalling Betty and deleting the %APPDATA%\betty and %APPDATA%\.betty folders removes all remaining data. You can also revoke Betty's access at any time from your Microsoft account settings (account.live.com/consent/Manage).

6. Children

Betty uses Microsoft accounts, so Microsoft's family and age settings apply to sign-in.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
