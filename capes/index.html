How do I use it in game?
  1. Press Download for Betty.
  2. Open the folder %APPDATA%\.betty\cosmetics (Win + R, paste, Enter).
  3. Extract the .zip there: it becomes a folder named after your cape.
  4. In game: Cosmetics › Capes. Betty loads it by itself, even while playing.
Your capes stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.
